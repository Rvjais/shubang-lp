About the Surgeon

Dr. Shubhang Aggarwal

MCh Orth (UK) · Senior Robotic Arthroplasty Surgeon

Dr. Shubhang Aggarwal introduced robotic knee replacement to North India in 2019 and heads the Department of Orthopaedics at NHS Hospital, Jalandhar — one of the region’s most quality-conscious joint replacement centres, offering comprehensive orthopaedic care under one roof.

His practice is built on a simple principle: a correct diagnosis comes before any operation. Many patients who visit are advised physiotherapy, biologics or cartilage-preserving procedures instead of a replacement — and are told so honestly.

The future in knee replacement is here. At NHS Hospital we have brought internationally acclaimed, cutting-edge robotic technology to orthopaedic and joint replacement surgery — the first such programme in the region.

1

3D Mapping of Your Knee

The robot builds a live 3D model of your joint in theatre — no pre-operative CT scan and no extra radiation.

Plan Balanced For You

A handheld robotic burr removes only the planned bone, protecting surrounding soft tissue and ligaments.

Frequently Asked

Questions Patients Actually Ask

Straight answers about robotic surgery, recovery, cost and whether you need an operation at all.

Often, no. Early and moderate arthritis frequently responds to weight management, structured physiotherapy, injections, biologic treatment or a cartilage-preserving procedure. A replacement is recommended only when the joint surface is genuinely worn out and daily life is affected. That is exactly why a proper diagnosis comes first — and why a second opinion costs you nothing here.

Conventional surgery uses standard jigs and the surgeon’s eye. Robotic surgery builds a 3D map of your knee in theatre, plans alignment and ligament balance specific to your anatomy, and then guides bone preparation to sub-millimetre accuracy. The result is a more precisely balanced knee, less soft-tissue trauma and typically a quicker, more comfortable early recovery.

Most patients stand and take their first supported steps the same day or the next morning, and manage stairs before discharge. Independent walking usually returns within two to three weeks, with normal daily activity by six to eight weeks. Your exact timeline depends on age, muscle condition and how consistently you follow the rehabilitation programme.

Yes, in suitable patients. Bilateral surgery in a single sitting means one anaesthetic, one hospital stay and one rehabilitation period. Whether it is right for you depends on your heart, lung and general fitness assessment, which is done before any decision is made.

High-flexion implants combined with accurate robotic alignment allow most patients to sit cross-legged and kneel for prayer comfortably. This is discussed openly before surgery so your expectations match what your particular knee and implant can deliver.

Cost depends on the implant chosen, whether one or both joints are treated, and your room category. Cashless treatment is available with most major insurers and TPAs, and our team helps with pre-authorisation paperwork. You are given a written estimate before admission — no surprises afterwards.
